Our Mission

Healthcare and Care for All

Founded in 2007 by Mujtaba Hasan Askari, HHF works tirelessly to bridge health inequity for the disadvantaged sections of society through direct interventions.

Accessible Medical Relief

Providing emergency medical relief, financial assistance for critical treatments (like cancer and dialysis), and essential medicines to those in dire need.

Empowerment Through Education

Supporting underprivileged students with scholarships, educational aid, and primary education to help them overcome obstacles and build successful careers.

Inclusivity and Transparency

We are a non-discriminatory organization serving all individuals, ensuring every donation directly impacts the beneficiary with complete transparency.

Comprehensive Care Verticals

From primary health centers and rehabilitation to specialized dialysis and geriatric care, we offer a wide spectrum of essential services.

What We Do

Our Key Programs & Initiatives

Rural Healthcare

Taking primary healthcare services directly to the doorsteps of remote villages where access is extremely limited.

Hospital Help Desks

Operating dedicated Help Desks in Government Hospitals to guide patients and strengthen the public health system.

Free Dialysis

Providing free, high-quality, life-saving dialysis sessions for economically disadvantaged CKD patients.

Educational Aid

Offering scholarships, school supplies, and vocational support to empower the youth in urban slums and rural areas.
